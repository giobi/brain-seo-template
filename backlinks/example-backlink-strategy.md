# Link Building Strategy - Sustainable Fashion

## Goals

- **Target:** 50 new backlinks from DR40+ sites
- **Timeline:** Q4 2025 (Oct-Dec)
- **Focus:** Sustainable fashion, ethical brands, eco lifestyle
- **Budget:** $500 (for tools, outreach assistant)

## Tactics

### 1. Guest Posting

**Target sites:**
- sustainablefashionblog.com (DR 55)
- ecowarrior.org (DR 50)
- ethicalfashionguide.com (DR 48)

**Topics to pitch:**
- "How to Transition to a Capsule Wardrobe"
- "5 Sustainable Fashion Myths Debunked"
- "The True Cost of Fast Fashion"

**Expected:** 10-15 backlinks

### 2. Resource Page Link Building

**Target:** "Sustainable fashion resources" pages

**Prospect list:**
- university.edu/sustainability/resources
- ecoblog.com/sustainable-living-resources
- fashionschool.edu/ethical-fashion-links

**Pitch:** Our comprehensive guides deserve inclusion

**Expected:** 5-10 backlinks

### 3. Broken Link Building

**Process:**
1. Find broken links on fashion/sustainability sites
2. Check if we have similar content
3. Reach out to webmaster

**Tools:** Ahrefs, Check My Links extension

**Expected:** 5-8 backlinks

### 4. Digital PR / Newsjacking

**Opportunities:**
- Fashion Week commentary
- Sustainability reports
- Industry statistics

**Targets:** Journalism sites, industry publications

**Expected:** 3-5 backlinks

### 5. Linkable Assets

**Create:**
1. **Infographic:** "The Journey of a Sustainable T-Shirt"
2. **Study:** "Survey of 1,000 Consumers on Sustainable Fashion"
3. **Calculator:** "Fashion Carbon Footprint Calculator"

**Promotion:** Outreach to bloggers, share on social, PR pitch

**Expected:** 10-15 backlinks per asset

### 6. Roundup Participation

**Target roundups:**
- "Best sustainable fashion blogs 2025"
- "Top experts on ethical fashion"
- "Sustainable living resources"

**Action:** Pitch ourselves for inclusion

**Expected:** 5-8 backlinks

### 7. Interview + Expert Quotes

**Platforms:**
- HARO (Help A Reporter Out)
- Terkel
- Featured

**Action:** Respond to queries on sustainable fashion

**Expected:** 5-10 backlinks

## Outreach Templates

### Template 1: Guest Post Pitch

```
Subject: Guest post idea: [Title]

Hi [Name],

I've been following [Site] and love your content on sustainable fashion. I noticed you recently published [article title] and thought I could contribute a complementary piece.

I'd like to propose: "[Title]" - an article covering [brief description].

I've written for [other sites] and can provide samples. The article would be 1,500+ words, SEO-optimized, with original images.

Interested?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Template 2: Resource Page Request

```
Subject: Resource suggestion for [Page Name]

Hi [Name],

I came across your excellent resource page on sustainable fashion: [URL]

I recently published a comprehensive guide that your readers might find valuable: [Your URL]

It covers [topics] with [unique value proposition].

Would you consider adding it to your resource list?

Thanks for considering!
[Your Name]
```

### Template 3: Broken Link Outreach

```
Subject: Broken link on [Page Name]

Hi [Name],

I was reading your article "[Title]" and noticed a broken link to [domain].

I have a similar resource that might work as a replacement: [Your URL]

It covers the same topic with updated information.

Hope this helps keep your content up to date!

Best,
[Your Name]
```
