# Backlinks

This folder contains link building strategy, outreach campaigns, and backlink monitoring.

## What Goes Here

- Link building strategies
- Outreach email templates
- Prospect lists
- Link acquisition tracking
- Backlink audit reports
- Disavow files (if needed)

## File Naming

Use campaign or strategy name:
- `guest-post-strategy.md`
- `outreach-campaign-2025-q4.md`
- `backlink-audit-2025-11.md`
- `linkable-assets-ideas.md`

## Template Structure

See [example-backlink-strategy.md](example-backlink-strategy.md) for a ready-to-use template.

## Tips

- Track outreach success rate (replied, agreed, published)
- Document what works and what doesn't
- Keep email templates for future campaigns
- Monitor acquired backlinks monthly
- Focus on quality over quantity (high DR, relevant sites)
