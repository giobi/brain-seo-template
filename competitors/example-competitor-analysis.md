# Competitor Analysis: Example.com

## Overview

**Website:** https://example.com
**Niche:** Sustainable fashion ecommerce
**Business Model:** DTC brand + content hub
**Domain Authority:** 65 (Moz)
**Estimated Traffic:** 500K/month (SEMrush)

## Strengths

### Content Strategy
- **Publishing frequency:** 8-10 blog posts per month
- **Content types:** Buying guides, brand reviews, sustainability news
- **Top performing content:**
  1. "Best Sustainable Fashion Brands 2025" (50K visits/month)
  2. "What is Sustainable Fashion? Complete Guide" (30K visits/month)
  3. "Sustainable vs Fast Fashion: The Truth" (25K visits/month)

### SEO Performance
- Strong rankings for commercial keywords
- Featured snippets for 15+ queries
- People Also Ask presence for top topics
- Rich snippets (FAQ schema) on key pages

### Technical SEO
- Fast page speed (95+ mobile score)
- Clean site structure
- Proper heading hierarchy
- Mobile-optimized

### Backlink Profile
- **Total backlinks:** 25,000+
- **Referring domains:** 1,200+
- **Top referring domains:**
  - fashionmagazine.com (DR 75)
  - sustainabilityblog.com (DR 60)
  - ecowarrior.org (DR 55)

## Weaknesses

### Content Gaps
- Limited coverage of:
  - Sustainable fashion certifications
  - DIY upcycling tutorials
  - Local sustainable brands (by region)
  - Price comparison guides

### Technical Issues
- No FAQ schema on blog posts
- Missing alt text on some images
- Some thin content pages (under 500 words)

### User Experience
- Newsletter popup too aggressive
- Long scroll to content (above-the-fold ads)

## Content to Beat

### Priority 1: Beat "Best Sustainable Fashion Brands 2025"
**Their URL:** example.com/best-brands-2025
**Word count:** 3,500 words
**Backlinks:** 150+

**How to beat it:**
- Longer, more comprehensive (4,000+ words)
- Video reviews embedded
- Interactive brand comparison tool
- More recent data (2025 vs 2024)
- Better internal linking

### Priority 2: Beat "What is Sustainable Fashion?"
**Their URL:** example.com/what-is-sustainable-fashion
**Word count:** 2,000 words
**Backlinks:** 200+

**How to beat it:**
- Add infographics (visual appeal)
- Include statistics and studies
- Expert quotes
- FAQ schema
- Related subtopics (circular fashion, slow fashion)

## Backlink Opportunities

**Where they got links from:**
1. Guest posts on fashion blogs
2. Interviews with founders
3. Resource pages (directories)
4. Sustainability roundups
5. Press coverage

**Tactics we can replicate:**
- Pitch guest posts to same blogs
- Create linkable assets (studies, infographics)
- Get listed in same directories
- Collaborate with eco influencers

## Strategy Recommendations

### Quick Wins
1. Target their content gaps (certifications, upcycling)
2. Improve our existing pages competing with them
3. Reach out to domains linking to them

### Long-term
1. Build authority in sub-niches they ignore
2. Create more comprehensive content for their top pages
3. Develop unique data/research they don't have

- [ ] Track new content published
